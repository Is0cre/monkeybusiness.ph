The Stance

Zero shortcuts. Clear glass is the proof.

Real fruit. Real rum. Absolutely not corporate. Mass-market liqueur is flavored and colored to imitate nature. We extract the living fruit into local rum in pure glassware with zero synthetic essence, dyes, or commercial stabilizers.

01 · PURITY

Clear Glass

No caramel coloring, no artificial clouding agents. If a spirit is pure, clear glass hides nothing.

02 · EXTRACTION

Cold-Process

Dual cold maceration preserves delicate aromatics and true fruit acidity that heat processing destroys.

03 · ORIGIN

Isabela, PH

Crafted between the Sierra Madre and the Cordilleras. The soil and the season define every drop.
