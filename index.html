The Foundation & Leadership

MARA

Managing Director & Co-Founder

Rooted in Cagayan Valley, Mara is the operational force and living heartbeat of Monkey Business.

Her standards were forged in the discipline of commercial food production—waking up before dawn to manage local bakeries, source fresh ingredients directly from regional growers, and command kitchen operations with zero tolerance for waste or cutting corners. In an industry dominated by impersonal corporate boardrooms, Mara anchors Monkey Business directly in the soil and culture of Isabela.

From hand-selecting peak-ripeness fruit at regional farm gates to leading our family tasting committee, every recipe, batch, and allocation moves under her direct authority. If a bottle doesn't meet her standard, it never leaves the estate.

The Workbench

Systems, Glassware & The Bloodline

Operating in the background to support Mara's vision is a commitment to pure physics and clinical hygiene.

Drawing from backgrounds in high-availability IT systems and biological laboratory protocols, our benchwork strictly eliminates industrial additives, commercial stabilizers, and metallic contact. We extract entirely in stationary Pyrex glassware, tracking osmotic curves and cold-crashing for crystal clarity.

Carrying the rebellious bloodline of L.O. Smith—who fought 19th-century monopolies for chemical purity—we provide the cold engineering so the real fruit can speak for itself.
